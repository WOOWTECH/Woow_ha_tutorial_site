Chapter 7

Notifications

Install the Home Assistant Companion App, connect your phone, and send a test notification. This completes the path from “Home Assistant detected something” to “you actually know about it.”

Why notifications matter

  • After washing in the washing machine → the phone dinged.
  • If the door is not closed → it will remind you when you go out 100 meters away.
  • The power outage is back → You at work will know first.
  • Camera captures person → Notifications with thumbnail.

Companion App is the carrier of these notifications, and it is also the most convenient tool for your daily HA operations.

The example notification in this chapter is trigger it (Manual test); the next chapter (ch8) teaches you how to notify Tuck in automation—For example, "Send a notification when the washing machine stops" and "Turn on the lights and notify when someone comes home."

Install Companion App

  1. Search "Home Assistant" in the phone store

    iOS: App Store search Home Assistant (Developed by Home Assistant).
    Android: Play store search Home Assistant Companion.

  2. Open it and select "Log in to Home Assistant"

    The App will automatically scan the regional network for HA hosts. If it is scanned, just click it; if it is not scanned, just enter it manually. http://homeassistant.local:8123 or your own URL.

What more will you get after installing the app?: In addition to push notifications, Companion App will automatically Battery level, location, WiFi name, Bluetooth sensor When the data is uploaded to HA, it becomes sensor.yourname_battery_level, device_tracker.yourname_phone etc. entity. These are the raw materials for the automation of "automatically turning on the lights when you get home" and "reminder when the battery is below 20%".

Log in with the account you just created

  1. Enter username and password

    use Chapter 5 Create a personal account (do not use a shared administrator account) - so notifications can be sent to you by name.

  2. Permissions to allow app access

    Will ask if I want to open it Notifications, location, Bluetooth, camera Wait. It is recommended to open at least:

    • Notifications — This is the focus of this chapter and must be opened.
    • location (always) — "Go home" and "leave home" the soul of automation.
    iOS reminder: The "Location: Always" of iOS needs to be changed in the system settings, and the first login can only be done "when using the app". Then go back to the settings and change it to "Forever".

  3. A bunch of sensors will automatically appear on the Home Assistant side.

    Go back to the HA web page → Settings → Devices and Services, and you will see a new device called your phone name (for example, "Xiao Ming's iPhone") with dozens of sensors: battery, charging, WiFi name, Focus mode, etc.

Send your first notification

This function has been renamed: The sidebar used to have an independent "Developer Tools", which will be moved into the whole package starting from 2026.2 Settings Here, in 2026.8, the name was simplified from “Developer Tools” to Tools. So the new version path is Settings → Tools → Actions, the old version is a sidebar Developer Tools → Actions, the paginated content inside is exactly the same. In addition, what was previously called "Service" will be officially renamed starting from 2024.8 Action, in YAML service: Also changed it to action:.

  1. Settings → Tools → Actions

    "Settings" → "Tools" → "Actions" on the left side of the HA web page.

  2. Select the action notify.mobile_app_XXX

    XXX is the name of the phone you just logged in to. This action is automatically created by the app for you.

    Notifications action naming rules: notify.mobile_app_<device name>, non-alphanumeric characters in the device name will become underscores. For example, iPhone 13 Pro Max will become notify.mobile_app_iphone_13_pro_max. Go to "Settings → Tools → Actions" and search notify.mobile_app You can see your phone.

  3. Fill in message and title

    Just give it a try:

    action: notify.mobile_app_xxx
data:
  title: "Test notification"
  message: "First message from HA"

  4. Click "Execute Action"

    The phone dings immediately! — Congratulations, your smart home now has a "mouth".

Verify: The phone should pop up a "test notification" within 5 seconds. If not received: (1) Is the App notification permission turned on? (2) The phone is not in Do Not Disturb mode. (3) The HA host is connected normally.

Advanced: Notifications with action buttons

Notifications are not just static text, they can also have buttons for you to click:

action: notify.mobile_app_xxx
data:
  title: "Someone rang the doorbell"
  message: "Tap an action below to choose whether to open the door."
  data:
    actions:
      - action: "OPEN_DOOR"
        title: "Open door"
      - action: "IGNORE"
        title: "Ignore"

Then listen for events in automation mobile_app_notification_action, get action: "OPEN_DOOR" Just perform the door opening action. Then you can open the door remotely no matter where you are in the world.

Two pitfalls that are easy to step into:
  1. Android can have up to 3 action buttons in one notification; iOS can be put to about 10 but more than that will require sliding down. The cross-platform limit is 3.
  2. action ID is unique in the entire domain. If you have two doorbells, use the same one action: OPEN_DOOR, clicking either will trigger the same automation. To avoid it, use {{ context.id }} Or prefixed with the device name, for example action: OPEN_DOOR_FRONT.

Tags and groups: manage multiple notifications

  • tag: laundry: The next notification with the same tag will be replace The previous one (for example, laundry progress: washing → spin → completed).
  • group: security: Notifications meeting of the same group stacked together (Doorbell + alarm are all in the same pile).
Advanced application notice: Camera action → Notifications with screenshot → There are action buttons "Record video for 30 seconds" / "Call the police". These are left for the next series of teachings.

FAQ

Notifications not received?
Check in order: (1) Whether the App notification permission of the phone system is turned on; (2) whether "Settings → Companion App → Notifications" in the App is turned on; (3) whether the name of the executed action is written correctly; (4) whether the phone is in Do Not Disturb mode.
Can all family members’ cell phones be notified?
Yes. There is a special action called notify.notify, will be sent to all Logged-in mobile app. You can also create your own notify group and send it to only some people.
Can I send via Email, Line, or Telegram?
Yes. SMTP, Telegram Bot, Line Notify (service has been discontinued and switched to Messaging API) are all integrated. However, it is recommended to familiarize yourself with the mobile app first, and use other channels as a backup.

Common problems

Not receiving notifications on phone
Check in order: (1) Companion App has enabled notification permission; (2) The phone is not in Airplane/Do Not Disturb/Power Saving mode; (3) HA external network is OK (App needs to connect to Firebase/APNs); (4) Is the action name correct? Go to "Settings → Tools → Actions" to search notify.mobile_app Do you have your cell phone?
iOS notification received but silent
iOS needs to set "Notifications Sounds" in the App and specify it in the notification data push: sound:. If not set, the display will be muted.
You can’t receive it away from home (you can receive it at home)
Companion App notifications are sent through Firebase/APNs, and will be received as long as the phone can access the Internet. It has nothing to do with whether you have Nabu Casa or DDNS. But to use the App to "operate" HA (not receive notifications), you need a remote connection - see the appendix.
