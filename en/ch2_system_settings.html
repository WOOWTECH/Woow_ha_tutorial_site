Chapter 2

System settings

Adjust the time zone, language, unit, and geographical location at once. These are the basis for HA to judge "sunrise and sunset", "typhoon approaching" and "how many degrees to turn on the air conditioner". If you set it wrong, it will be wrong later.

Why do you need to set this up first?

Many of HA's automations rely on "geographic location" and "time zone":

  • "Turn on the porch lights 30 minutes after sunset" - need to know your location.
  • (For example: If you write the automation of "automatically turning on the lights when the sun goes down" in the future, setting the wrong latitude and longitude here will cause the lights to turn on at the wrong time.)
  • "27°C with air conditioning on" - Celsius or Fahrenheit? If the unit is wrong, the judgment will be wrong.

There isn't much to do in this chapter, but it affects every subsequent chapter.

The consequences of setting the wrong time zone are greater than imagined: The time on the historical chart will be distorted, the sunrise and sunset triggers will be wrong, and the energy dashboard will return to the wrong day. The geographical location itself only affects sunrise and sunset zone.home(HA will build a "home" zone with a radius of 100 meters according to the coordinates you marked),The time zone, unit, and currency must be manually selected separately., will not guess automatically.

Enter settings

Click "Settings" (gear icon) on the left menu. This is the entrance to all system settings of HA.

Hands-on setup

Note: This English edition generalizes the zh-TW edition’s Taiwan-specific example. Check the services, regulations, frequencies, prices, and availability in your region. The main battlefield of this chapter is now calledSettings → System → Home information, official documents have been changed to this name. The same page on the old version of the screen is called "General", and the positions and fields are exactly the same. The screenshot below is of the old version of the interface.

  1. Settings → System → Home Information

    Click "System" on the settings page, then click "Family Information" (called "General" in the old version). This page is the main battlefield of this chapter.

  2. Set the name and location of your "home"

    Name: For example, "Wang Family" and "Chen Family" - this name will be displayed in the mobile phone notification title.

    location: Drag the pin directly on the map to your doorstep. This is the starting point of the "at home/not at home" judgment.

  3. Time zones and currencies

    Time zone: select your local IANA time zone; the zh-TW source uses Asia/Taipei. Currency: select your local currency; the source example is TWD. Both settings affect timestamps and the Energy dashboard.

  4. unit system

    unit system: Select "Metric".temperature:Celsius.rainfall: mm.wind speed:m/s or km/h.

  5. save

    Click "Save" in the lower right corner. After modification, some settings will not take effect until the page is reset.

    Tip:If you later feel that the sunrise and sunset times are weird or the notification time is wrong, it is probably because there is a missing field on this page.
Verify: After saving, return to "Settings → Overview". The date and time in the lower right corner should show your local time zone; the map in "Settings → Areas, labels & zones → Zones → home" should frame your home.

Personal preferences (own avatar, language, theme)

Concept: system settings vs personal settings. Time zone, unit, currency aresystem level(shared by everyone); language, date format, first day of week arePersonal level(Separate settings for each account). This is why family members often see different languages ​​when logging in, so don’t think it’s broken.

Just now it was the setting for "whole home", here is the setting for "yourself": click your name at the bottom of the sidebar → "User profile". The old version was called "User Settings".

  1. language

    Select "Traditional Chinese" and the entire interface will be immediately Chinese-language.

  2. Time format and initial page

    time format: 24-hour or 12-hour viewing habits.Default dashboard: Which page do you want to see after logging in (the default is "Overview").

  3. Theme (Theme, the old version of the interface is translated as "scenery")

    The drop-down menu has several built-in themes, including "Backend-selected" (following the system's dark and light colors), "Default light/dark", etc.

FAQ

After changing the time zone, will the past chart time disappear?
Yes. Historical data is stored in UTC, and the time zone is applied when displayed, so the image you see after changing the time zone will shift. This is correct behavior, don't worry.
Does the location of “home” have to be real?
It is best to use real coordinates, because sunrise and sunset, weather, and earthquake notifications all rely on it. If you care about privacy, set up at a landmark within a 1 kilometer radius.
Can different users choose their own languages?
Yes. The language set by the system is only the "default value", and each user can choose it on the "User Profile" page.
