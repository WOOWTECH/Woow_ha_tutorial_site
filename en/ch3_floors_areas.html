Chapter 3

Floors and areas

First "draw" in HA how many floors your home has and what rooms are on each floor. After the drawing is completed, the control, dashboard, and voice assistant will know which lights are "turning on the lights in the living room on the first floor."

Why paint a house?

HA uses three levels to describe the physical space of your home:

  • Floor: First floor, second floor, basement, top floor.
  • Area: Living room, master bedroom, kitchen, garage.
  • Device/Entity: Specific light bulbs, switches, and sensors are placed in the area.

After drawing the floors and areas, you will immediately get these abilities:

  • Voice: "Hey Google, turn off all the lights on the first floor" - HA knows which lights belong to the first floor.
  • Dashboard: Automatically generate a "paged by region" view.
  • Automation: The conditions for "there is movement in the master bedroom" can be written out.

Four levels of stairs (remember this structure):

  • Floor: Only regions can be installed, equipment cannot be installed directly. Example: 1st floor, 2nd floor.
  • Area: Room unit, belonging to one floor. Example: living room, master bedroom.
  • Device: Belongs to a region (can be overwritten). Example: Xiaomi light bulb.
  • Entity: An angle at which the device can be operated, and it only belongs to one area (default follows the device). Example:light.living_room_main.

a device/entityCan only belong to one area, an areaCan only belong to one floor.

Current status inspection

Go to "Settings → Regions, Labels and Blocks" to see the current status. You may see three situations:

  1. All empty — A new installation is the easiest, just follow this chapter and start from scratch.
  2. Only areas without floors — The old version of HA does not have the concept of floors. Follow this chapter to add floors.
  3. There are floors but it’s confusing — I tried it before but didn’t organize it. Consider deleting it and starting over.
Page name:Floors and zones are set on the page "Settings → Areas, labels & zones"; the old version before 2024.4 did not have floors and labels, and this page only listed the zones. To put the device into the area, go to "Settings → Devices and Services → Devices". This path has not been renamed so far. In addition, the default dashboard in the sidebar is called "Home" starting from 2026.2, and the old version is called "Overview".

Think about the structure first (just take 5 minutes)

It is recommended to list on paper or in your head first:

  • How many floors are there? Does the basement count?
  • What “spaces would you mention using voice or automation” on each floor?
  • Should the "balcony", "porch" and "corridor" be separate areas?
Naming suggestions:useNames you say every day. If you say "turn on the master bedroom light", call it "Master Bedroom", not "Bedroom_M". Chinese is no problem for the voice assistant.
Named counterexample:Do not use abbreviations (LR), code name (Zone_A), plural (Bedrooms) — and you yourself will forget which is which later.

Build it yourself

  1. Add new floor

    "+ Create Floor" in the upper right corner of the page → enter "First Floor" → click on the icon bar, the icon selector will pop up, search floor You can see the floor related icon → Save. Repeatedly create "second floor", "basement", etc.

  2. The purpose of floor number

    Floor number used for sorting: Basement -1, first floor 1, second floor 2, top floor 10. It will then be displayed on the dashboard and cards in this order.

  3. Add new area

    Click "+ Create Area" under a certain floor → enter "living room" → select the icon (mdi:sofa) → Save.

  4. List of common areas (can be copied directly)

    first floor: Entrance, living room, dining room, kitchen, guest bathroom, back balcony.

    second floor: Master bedroom, master bath, children's room, study room, corridor.

    outdoor: Yard, garage, gate.

    Tip:You can also build an "outdoor" area without a designated floor, or you can open an "outdoor" floor yourself.
Verify: Go back to "Settings → Areas, Labels and Blocks", you should see the newly created floors/areas filtered on the left; click on any area, and the number of devices in this area will be listed on the right.

Put the device into the zone

It’s not enough to have an empty frame, you have to put the device in it:

  1. Go to "Settings → Devices and Services → Devices"

    See a list of all paired devices.

  2. Click on a device → Edit (pencil) in the upper right corner → Select area

    The drop-down menu will list all the areas you just created, select one to save.

  3. Batch designation (multiple devices together)

    There is "Batch Selection" in the upper right corner of the device list - you can "move to area" at once after checking multiple ones. I just installed an entire room of lights and this feature is a great time saver.

Next step: After the area is assigned, Chapter 4 teaches you how to move each device from switch.0x00abc123 Change the name to something understandable and use labels to group across regions.

Completion of comparison

After you're done, when you return to the "Regions, Labels and Blocks" page, it should look like this:

FAQ

Can I change the floor/area name later?
Yes. Changing the name will not affect the device connection, nor will it break the automation (automation uses the internal ID). However, the display name will be changed simultaneously throughout the site.
Can a device belong to two areas at the same time?
No. A device can only belong to one zone. If a lamp is in both the "living room" and "dining room" open spaces, choose the most important one.
If I delete an area, will the devices in it disappear?
No, the device is still there, but it has become an "unspecified area". You can specify it back at any time.
Doesn’t every room need to have a zone?
No. Only the rooms you will "use" are needed. There is no need to build any smart devices in the storage room. It will be added later when new equipment needs to be installed.

Common stuck levels

What should I do if I accidentally put my device in the wrong area?
Go back to "Settings → Devices and Services → Devices" to find the device, click on it and there is a "Region" drop-down in the upper right corner to change it. Entities can also be overwritten individually (after overwriting, they will be separated from the device).
There is already a bunch of equipment, is it too late to re-plan the area?
In time, regional classification will not affect automation (as long as automation writes entity_id, not device_id). Ch4 will talk about naming, and ch8 will talk about why it is safe to use entity_id.
