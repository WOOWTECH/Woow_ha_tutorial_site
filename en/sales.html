Why ask WoowTech to set it up?

Home Assistant supports a broad range of smart-home systems and has an active global community. That flexibility also creates a learning curve: supporting many systems means making many choices. Selecting hardware, flashing an image, commissioning Zigbee or Matter devices, building automations, and managing upgrades all require time and ongoing attention.

  • Time: Reading documentation, comparing recommendations, and troubleshooting can take many hours.
  • Reliability: An overlooked breaking change can interrupt automations throughout the home.
  • Security: Carelessly exposing port 8123 can make Home Assistant reachable from the public internet.
Key idea: You may want the benefits of a smart home without maintaining every component yourself. WoowTech is defining setup and managed-service options. [Owner: WoowTech Product — approve the service scope and availability before publication.]

What could setup include?

These topics come from the 22-chapter Home Assistant Onboarding Guide. They illustrate possible work; they do not define an included service package. [Owner: WoowTech Product — approve the final service catalog.]

System and environment

  • Time zone, language, units, and location
  • Floors and areas
  • Entity names, labels, and categories
  • Household and guest accounts, permissions, and MFA

Add-ons and integrations

  • Zigbee2MQTT or ZHA for Option A (self-hosted)
  • ESPHome, Node-RED, and Studio Code Server
  • File editor and Terminal & SSH for administrative work
  • MQTT broker, InfluxDB, or MariaDB for advanced use
  • Weather, calendars, notifications, and Companion App setup

Initial automations

  • Entry light at sunset and night-light control at sunrise
  • Arrival behavior after everyone has been away
  • Good-night controls for lights, climate devices, and locks
  • Low-battery and offline-device notifications
  • Scheduled backups and backup-status notifications

Remote access and security

  • A Nabu Casa, Cloudflare Tunnel, or WireGuard design approved for the deployment
  • Trusted proxies and reverse-proxy configuration where applicable
  • MFA and other agreed protective controls
  • No direct public exposure of port 8123
