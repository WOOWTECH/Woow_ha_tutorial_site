Why ask WoowTech to set it up?

Home Assistant is one of the most open and active smart-home platforms. That flexibility is also its learning curve: supporting almost anything means making many choices yourself. Hardware selection, image flashing, Zigbee or Matter commissioning, automation design, and upgrades all carry a time and maintenance cost.

  • Time: Documentation, videos, and troubleshooting can consume many hours.
  • Reliability: An unnoticed breaking change can disrupt automations throughout the home.
  • Security: Exposing port 8123 carelessly is like leaving a house key in public.
Concept: You want to use a smart home, not become its full-time engineer. WoowTech can handle installation, maintenance, and remote access so that you can remain the user.

FAQ

How much does it cost? Can you indicate a range first?
The construction fee varies greatly depending on the depth of hardware and add-ons; the monthly rent is divided into three levels based on family size. We do not put the price list on the website, because the estimate of "what your home actually needs" is meaningful - please use the contact channel once it is confirmed, and we can give you an accurate quotation after a 30-minute consultation.
I already have HA running, can I take over?
Yes, this is a very common situation. WoowTech will make a "current health inspection report" - which settings are unsafe, which add-ons should be replaced, and whether there are any upgrade minefields - you decide whether to entrust us to take over the maintenance. After entrustment, your HA will enter our maintenance schedule and enjoy the same treatment as machines shipped directly from Option A.
Is installation or delivery available in my country?
On-site installation, shipping, travel charges, and online onboarding vary by country [SERVICE AREA AND FEES TO BE CONFIRMED]. The machines in Option A are all set up in our factory before being shipped out. You can start using them just by plugging them in and connecting them to the Internet. The remote connection allows us to assist you as if someone is right next to you.
What should I do if I move?
Option A: Take the host with you and restore it when the new home is plugged in. If the network environment changes and you need to reset the remote connection, WoowTech remote assistance can complete it within 30 minutes. Option B: The cloud solution has nothing to do with the physical address. You don’t have to do anything when moving, just change the Zone’s address in HA to your new home.
Will WoowTech peek into my home information?
No, and the contract states there are penalties. Option A: We need to use the short-term authorization channel to connect, and you can revoke it at any time; Option B: By default, we cannot log in to your instance. If you need to help you handle it, you need to issue an invoice and authorize it in the customer service system - every operation will leave an audit record, which you can export at any time.
Can I just buy the hardware and not set it up?
Yes, we are also resellers of official HA hardware. However, if you just want to save the setup fee, our experience is that most people will still come back to entrust us after 3 months - the time cost is much higher than the solution fee. Pure hardware purchases will come with the complete tutorial of "HA Onboarding Guide". If you are sure you want to learn, this path will fully support you.
What’s the difference between Nabu Casa’s official cloud and your Option B?
Nabu Casa "helps you add cloud connection and voice to your home HA"; WoowTech Option B is "even the HA host runs directly on the cloud", and you don't even need to buy hardware. The two positions are different: if you already have HA at home and want to connect remotely with Google/Alexa voice, choose Nabu Casa; if you don’t even want to buy the hardware and want to start right away, choose Option B.
